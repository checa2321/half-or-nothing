What to Check Before You Buy

Gucci sunglasses are among the most copied on any marketplace, so the model code is your best tool. Genuine frames carry a code, usually starting with GG followed by digits, printed inside the temple along with the lens and bridge sizes and a color code. Search that code and compare it to Gucci's own listing for the same model.

Look at the packaging too. Authentic pairs normally come with a case and a cleaning cloth, but the details vary by production year, so treat them as one signal and not as proof. Sellers who show the inside of the temple in their photos are easier to trust than sellers who only show a catalog image.

Prices on this page are compared with each seller's own stated original price. We do not verify authenticity, so before you buy, read the condition line, check the seller's feedback, and confirm they accept returns. Our designer sunglasses guide walks through what to check, and you can browse every brand we list on the brands page.
