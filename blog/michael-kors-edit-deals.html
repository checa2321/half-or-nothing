Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between Michael Kors Collection and regular Michael Kors?

Michael Kors Collection is the brand's runway line, shown at New York Fashion Week. It's priced and made closer to a European luxury house than the mainline label sold at department stores. The mainline, sometimes labeled MICHAEL Michael Kors, is the more accessible line most shoppers know.

Why are these prices so much lower than the original retail price?

These are real end-of-season and overstock listings from verified eBay sellers, not counterfeit goods sold at a discount. Retail prices on runway-tier pieces like the Collection dresses above are set for department store and boutique markups. Once a season ends, sellers move remaining stock at a steep cut to clear it.
