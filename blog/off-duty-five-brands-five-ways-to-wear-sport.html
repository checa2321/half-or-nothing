OFF/DUTY: Five Brands. Five Ways to Wear Sport.

2026-11-15 · Written by Dann Nazario · 9 min read

Sportswear stopped belonging to the gym a long time ago. These five brands each found a different way out of it: Adidas through the track, Puma through the court, Reebok through the tennis club, Skechers through comfort and Champion through the campus. Here is one way into each of them, starting with the piece that carries the most history.

Key Takeaways

  • One pick per brand to start with: the Adidas Ultraboost 5X, the Puma Suede, the Reebok Club C 85, the Skechers S Sport Brennen 2.0 and the Champion Reverse Weave hoodie.
  • Each brand section pairs shoes with clothes, except Champion, which is clothing and bags in this edit.
  • Prices and discounts live on the product cards and can change, so check the card before you buy.
  • Several listings have limited sizes and stock.

Let’s be real. As much as I love a beautiful pair of heels, a gorgeous blazer, and everything that comes with dressing up, there are days when the only thing I want is a good pair of sneakers, a comfortable hoodie, and absolutely nothing that requires ironing.

And honestly, who said being comfortable meant sacrificing style? I love that a pair of sneakers can look just as good with a blazer as it does with an oversized sweatshirt. There is something liberating about getting dressed without taking the whole thing too seriously.

So, this time, I wanted to play a little with five brands, five different personalities, and that wonderful space where sportswear meets everyday fashion. Whether you are heading to the gym, catching a flight, grabbing coffee, or simply enjoying a day with absolutely no plans, this one’s for you.

Because looking good and feeling comfortable should never be mutually exclusive. And neither should having great taste and being smart about what you spend.

Are these Adidas, Puma, Reebok, Skechers and Champion pieces really at half off or more?

Can I mix pieces from different brands?

When did the Reebok Club C launch?

According to Reebok's archive, the Club C launched in 1985 as a tennis shoe for the club player.

What is Champion Reverse Weave?

It is a construction method where the fabric is cut cross-grain to reduce shrinkage. Champion first patented it in 1938, and the patent was finalized in 1952.

Why do some listings only show one size?

Deeply discounted listings often have limited stock, and the card reflects the size and color currently on offer. If your size is gone, check the full product page for other options.
