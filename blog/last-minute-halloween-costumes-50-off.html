A Quick Safety Check

A costume bought in a hurry still needs a few checks. The U.S. Consumer Product Safety Commission advises wearing a costume that fits and skipping overly long or baggy pieces, which can cause trips and falls. It also says the eye and nose holes in a mask should allow full visibility and adequate breathing, and that makeup may be a safer alternative to a mask. If you want that option, see our makeup deals. For trick-or-treating after dark, the agency suggests reflective tape on costumes and a flashlight or glow stick.

Source: CPSC, "CPSC Offers Safety Tips to Keep Hazards from Haunting Your Halloween," October 18, 2022.
