How we checked

On November 21, 2026 we looked up each Amazon product on Amazon and compared its current price with the list price Amazon shows. We kept products at 50% or more under that price that were new, in stock and sold with a buy box. The eBay listings come from our live deal feed, and the page rebuilds their prices from the listing, so one that sells out or drops under 50% off is removed. We left out a group of toasters and kettles from unfamiliar sellers whose list prices looked inflated.
