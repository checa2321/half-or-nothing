What It Is About, No Spoilers

My Take on the Series

The women in this series are incredible, and their love is beautiful and dreamlike. Robyn is one of the best characters I have read, a beautiful devotion. I gave The Echoes of Her Silence 4.7 stars: it is a super slow burn and it gets hot. The Gravity of Her Love is a 5-star favorite, a roller coaster of emotions with a perfect plot and a love story that is as romantic as it is hot. If you end up loving Skye Von Triessen as much as I do, my other favorite series of hers is Meant to Be, with Alexandra and Blake, one of my favorite couples I have ever read. Their love feels so pure and loyal, and the chemistry is amazing. It is also included with Kindle Unlimited.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to read Part 1 before this one?

No. You can read the two posts in any order. Part 1 has different books, with one exception: Windburn, the first book of the Crow's Coven series, appears in both posts, here next to its sequel, Firewild.

What is the difference between the deals and the watch list?

The deals are books we found at 50% off or more on the day we checked. The watch list holds favorites that have not reached that discount yet, and I will keep checking them.

What ages are the young adult picks for?

Amazon lists Not My Problem for ages 13 and up and The Lesbiana's Guide to Catholic School for ages 15 and up. Check the content warnings on StoryGraph first if the reader is young.

Can I start with Alchemy and a Cup of Tea?

It is the fourth and final book of the Tomes & Tea series, and the publisher describes it as the cozy close of the series. If you are new to Reyna and Kianthe, you may prefer to start with book 1, which is also available in Spanish as De traiciones y té. The deal here is on the deluxe edition of book 4.
