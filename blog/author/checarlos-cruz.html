eBay & Amazon

eBay. Rescanned hourly. Sorted purely by discount, and nothing under 50% is listed. No countdown timers, no invented scarcity: every number on this page is measured, not staged. See How We Verify Deals for the full methodology.

Amazon works differently. The Amazon Picks section is hand-picked by Che, one at a time. When a pick shows a price, list price, or coupon, it was verified by hand directly on Amazon -- not pulled from an automated scan -- see About for why.

Affiliate disclosure & how this page is built
Some links on this site are affiliate links. This site is a member of the eBay Partner Network. If you buy through one, I may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. Commission never affects which deals are listed or the order they appear in. Listings default to newest first; sort by discount, price, or name any time. Original prices and discount percentages are set by the seller and shown as reported by the retailer's API; Half or Nothing does not independently verify them. As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases.
Pricing & how often this updates

Prices and availability change at the retailer. Whatever the retailer shows at the time of purchase is what applies.

The "% off" shown on each card is the discount the seller lists against their own stated original price — it comes straight from the retailer's data, not from our own price history, and we don't independently verify it.
