The tailoring

Start with the jacket. Ralph Lauren tailoring is deliberately unfussy: a natural shoulder, two buttons, a lapel that sits where it should. The Lauren Ralph Lauren line is the workhorse, cut in wool that presses sharp and holds up for years of weddings and offices. The Polo label sits a step above it, trimmer through the chest and finished with the details you only catch up close.

Frequently asked questions

Is this Ralph Lauren authentic?

Every item in this edit is listed new: the clothing new with tags, the fragrances new with box. Every listing also ships through eBay with its Money Back Guarantee, which covers you if an item arrives and is not as described. Ralph Lauren is a heavily counterfeited label, so check the seller's feedback and photos before you buy, the same as you would anywhere.

What is the difference between Lauren Ralph Lauren and Polo Ralph Lauren?

Lauren Ralph Lauren is the department-store line for women and men, priced for everyday. Polo Ralph Lauren is the older, more expensive sportswear label with the pony logo. Both are genuine Ralph Lauren, and both show up in this edit.

What do Eau de Parfum, Parfum, and Elixir mean?

They are concentration levels. Eau de Toilette is the lightest, then Eau de Parfum, then Parfum and Elixir, which hold the most fragrance oil and last the longest. Every scent on this page is one of the stronger three.
