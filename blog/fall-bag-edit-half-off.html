Written by Dann Nazario

Fall 2026 bag coverage keeps circling the same handful of shapes. Vogue's fall handbag guide gives east-west its own section and lists Fendi's baguette in it. Coveteur lists east-west bags and top-handle bowling bags among its five trends, and Who What Wear says the season is getting "slouchier and more lived-in." No single outlet lists exactly the same four shapes, so we grouped the edit ourselves: the slouchy hobo, the baguette, the top handle, and the long, low east-west tote. We went shape by shape and pulled 15 pieces that are live right now at half off or more, with no rounding up.

Key Takeaways

  • East-west is the deepest category here, 6 of the 15 pieces, and Vogue's fall guide titles its section "East West Is Here to Stay."
  • The 15 pieces range from $62 to $1,282 and cover suede, soft leather, nylon, and woven ribbon.
  • Browns, creams, and stone are the easy neutrals on this list, from the Burberry suede tote to the Sak hobo to the Michael Kors striped tote.
  • The baguette is real, but thin: only two pieces cleared our 50% bar across eBay and Amazon combined. We're listing what's actually there instead of stretching the section to five.
  • Every price and link below comes from the seller's own live listing on the day this posted, not an estimate.

Which Shape Should You Actually Buy

If you are only adding one bag this fall, buy for the gap in your closet, not the trend ranking. If everything you own already hangs at the hip, the east-west tote changes your silhouette the most for the money. Start with the $62 Michael Kors Jet Set to test the shape before committing to it. For one bag that reads more expensive than it costs, the top-handle category has the widest spread here, from $74 to $650, so the investment framing only holds at the top of that range. And if you already have a hobo and a tote, skip the baguette for now. Two real options is a watchlist, not a category.

A Quick Care Note by Material

Only one piece here is suede, the Burberry tote. Water can mark suede for good, so treat it with a suede protector spray before the first wear, not after the first rain. Canvas and nylon totes handle daily wear better than leather but show dirt at the corners first, and a dry microfiber cloth beats any wet cleaning. Leather pieces generally do better with occasional conditioning and a dust bag. Check each listing's material line before you buy.
