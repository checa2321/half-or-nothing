How to Tell a Christmas Sale Is Actually Real

A "50% off" badge is only worth something if the original price was real to begin with. Before anything goes on this page, we check the listed price against the brand's own site or a comparable major retailer, not just against a number the seller picked. Frequently Asked Questions

What are some good Christmas gifts this year?

Anything from a category the recipient already reaches for on their own, at a price that would normally be out of range. A watch, a real fragrance, or a piece they'd hesitate to buy at full price all work, especially at 50% off or more.

What is the 5 gift rule for Christmas?

A guideline for gift-giving that says to buy something the person wants, something they need, something to wear, something to read, and one more thing. It keeps a gift list from becoming either one expensive item or a pile of small ones.

What are the most popular Christmas presents?

Watches, fragrance and accessories consistently rank as the most-searched Christmas gifts, since they work across age and relationship without needing a size or a strong personal taste.

How do I know a Christmas discount is real?

Check the listed price against the same or a comparable item on the brand's own site or a major retailer. A real 50% off discount holds up against that comparison. See our full breakdown in How to Verify a Real Online Deal.

Is this inventory still available after Christmas?

Everything above was verified live at publish time, but resale inventory on eBay turns over by the hour. If a specific listing sells out, the category page it came from will have similar items at a similar discount.
