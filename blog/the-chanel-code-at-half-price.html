So I decided to look beyond the label and focus on what makes the look so recognizable. Think tweed and bouclé jackets, quilted chain bags, and classic slingbacks, all from other designers and all at 50% off or more.

Of course, I couldn’t leave Chanel out entirely. I included a Chanel Vanity Case listing that caught my eye, along with the 2.55, an iconic design I’d love to see drop in price someday. We’ve found a few more Chanel pieces too, just none at 50% off yet.

Because sometimes, finding the look you love doesn’t mean paying for the name on the label. And at Half or Nothing, that’s exactly the kind of opportunity I’m always looking for.

01Why Chanel, Right Now

Matthieu Blazy's second runway collection for Chanel put the house's icons front and center again. The Zoe Report says he riffed on the brand's well-known icons, tweed jackets, flap bags and footwear with the double C, and that the skirt suit showed up more than 20 times. The South China Morning Post describes the fall pre-collection the same way: signatures like the tweed suit turned into easy, wearable staples. The latest show, his spring-summer 2027 collection, took place in Paris on October 5 with Nicole Kidman, Dua Lipa and Pedro Pascal in the audience, according to the AP.
