Frequently Asked Questions

What makes a loafer or oxford count as "premium" here?

The brand history and construction quality, not just the price before the discount. Ferragamo, Isaia, and Saint Laurent build with full-grain leather and traditional shoemaking techniques (hand-finished waxing, Neapolitan construction) that differ from mass-market dress shoes even before a discount is applied.

What is the real difference between a loafer, an oxford, and a derby?

A loafer has no laces and slips on. An oxford has closed lacing, where the eyelet facings are stitched under the vamp, which is what makes it the more formal of the two laced styles. A derby has open lacing, with the facings stitched on top, which makes it slightly less formal and a little easier to fit different foot shapes.

Are these real discounts off the actual retail price?

Yes. Every price and percentage in this post is pulled directly from the live eBay listing at the time of writing, checked against the item's stated original retail price. Half or Nothing only features items at 50 percent off or more.
