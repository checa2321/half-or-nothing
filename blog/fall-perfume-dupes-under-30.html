Key Takeaways

  • Five fall and winter scents, all under $30 right now: vanilla, oud, cinnamon, creamy rose and spiced apple, the notes that work best once the weather turns.
  • Each one is named next to a luxury original that costs $125 to $435 at the brand's own store.
  • Honest closeness ratings: some reviewers call a pair nearly identical, others say it is the same vibe, not a copy. We show both sides.
  • A dupe is its own perfume, sold under its own name, with a similar scent profile. It is not a counterfeit.

Cold weather changes what smells good. Bright citrus and aquatic summer scents fade fast in dry air. Vanilla, amber, spice and woods get warmer and last longer, on skin and on a wool coat. The catch is price. The most talked-about fall fragrances are niche bottles: Parfums de Marly lists Althaïr from $290 for 75 ml, and Initio lists Oud for Greatness at $435 for 90 ml.

These five get named next to those bottles again and again. All five are live on eBay at 50% off or more as we write this. If you missed our first two rounds, we covered women's and unisex perfume dupes and men's perfume dupes checked against Fragrantica. None of the five below repeats a bottle from those posts.

How to Wear a Fall Scent So It Lasts

  • Spray on pulse points and clothes. Warm notes like vanilla, amber and oud cling to wool and cotton and can last longer there than on dry skin.
  • Moisturize first. Fragrance holds better on hydrated skin, and cold, dry air makes scents fade faster.
  • Go lighter with the loud ones. 9PM and Oud for Glory project strongly. One or two sprays is plenty indoors.
  • Layer with intent. A single spray of Liquid Brun under Noble Blush adds a vanilla base to the rose without hiding it.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a perfume dupe?

A perfume dupe is a different fragrance, sold under its own name and brand. It is built around a scent profile similar to a more expensive original. It is legal and is not a counterfeit, which would copy the original's name and packaging.

Which fall perfume dupe is closest to the original?

French Avenue Liquid Brun has the strongest comparison here. A reviewer who owns Parfums de Marly Althaïr called the first 1 to 2 hours completely identical, with Liquid Brun fading faster after that.

Is Oud for Glory the same as Oud for Greatness?

No. They share a woody, spicy oud profile, and one side-by-side test called them about 95% identical in the dry down. Other reviewers find Oud for Glory more synthetic in the opening and less refined.

Are these perfumes good for women?

Yes. Noble Blush and Teriaq are made for women and Liquid Brun is unisex. Oud for Glory and 9PM are listed as men's fragrances on Parfumo. Sellers often market Oud for Glory as unisex, and 9PM's sweet vanilla base is easy for anyone to wear.

How long do these fall dupes last?

Reviewers describe all five as long-lasting. One Parfumo reviewer put Liquid Brun at about 10 hours, and 9PM and Oud for Glory are known for strong projection. Results vary with skin and weather.

Why are these perfumes so cheap on eBay?

Houses like Lattafa, Afnan and French Avenue price their perfumes far below niche brands. Sellers often discount them further. Check the live price on each card, since deals change fast.
