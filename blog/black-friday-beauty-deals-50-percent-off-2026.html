How we checked

On November 21, 2026 we looked up each product on Amazon and compared its current price with the list price Amazon shows. We kept only products at 50% or more under that list price that were new, in stock and sold with a buy box. A product that was out of stock, had no list price, or sat below 50% was dropped, which is why several Medicube and Wavytalk products from the Black Friday headlines are not here.
