Halloween is the one holiday where Americans spend real money on things that live in a closet eleven months a year. The National Retail Federation expects Halloween spending to reach $13.5 billion in 2026, with 74% of people planning to celebrate. Half of consumers plan to dress in costume and 42% plan to decorate their home or yard (source: NRF).

That's a lot of plastic ghosts bought at full price. So I went through both places we track deals, Amazon and eBay. I kept only what was 50% off or more on October 3, 2026, checked live on the actual product pages.

The Amazon side is the part you'd expect: decorations and costumes. The eBay side is where it got fun. There's a real perfume line literally called Halloween, and a whole category of watches named after the skeleton.

How I Picked These

The check happened on Saturday, October 3, 2026. On Amazon I was signed in and shipping to a Texas ZIP code, since Amazon can show different prices by delivery location.

On Amazon, every product had to be 50% off or more against the list price on the page, rated 4.2 stars or higher, and in stock. All but two have more than 100 reviews. The black cat basket has 73 (4.6 stars) and the ghost bandana has 84 (4.7 stars). I cut anything with an "only 2 left" warning and anything sitting at exactly 50%. Of the 18 Amazon listings I analyzed, 14 made it. A second search just for pet costumes added one more.

On eBay, I opened each listing to confirm it was still live at the same price. I only kept sellers with large feedback histories. Leperfect, Perfume Empire, Officialwatchdeals and Jomashop each show tens of thousands of ratings or more.

One safety note for kids' costumes. Frequently Asked Questions

When should I buy Halloween decorations and costumes?

As early as you can. Discounts like these sell through, and shipping times stretch out in the last two weeks of October. Everything on this list was 50% off or more on October 3, 2026.

Is the Halloween perfume a novelty or a real fragrance?

It's a real fragrance line. Jesús del Pozo launched the original Halloween perfume in 1997, and it became a best-seller with many flankers since (source: Fragrantica).

Are the eBay prices guaranteed?

No. eBay listings can sell out or change price at any time. Our blog checks eBay prices on a schedule and hides a listing if it sells out or drops below 50% off.

What is a skeleton watch?

A watch whose dial is cut away to show the mechanical movement inside. All three skeleton watches on this list are automatics, meaning they wind themselves from the motion of your wrist.
