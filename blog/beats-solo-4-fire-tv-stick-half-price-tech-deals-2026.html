How we checked

On November 21, 2026 we looked up each Amazon product on Amazon and compared its current price with the list price Amazon shows. We kept products at 50% or more under that price that were new, in stock and sold with a buy box. A product that was out of stock, had no list price, or sat below 50% was dropped, and so were listings from unfamiliar sellers whose list prices looked inflated. The two eBay listings come from our live deal feed, and the page rebuilds their prices from the listing, so one that sells out or drops under 50% is removed.
