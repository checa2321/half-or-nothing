2026-08-24 · Written by Checarlos Cruz · 4 min read

The pattern worth noticing

None of these six real percentages match the discount badge the seller shows. That's expected, their number is against a claimed original price, ours is against the highest price we've personally watched the item hold. Neither number is more “official” than the other. The difference is that we can show you ours happened, check by check, with real dates attached. That's the whole reason this tracking exists.

Frequently asked questions

How is a "real drop" different from the discount a seller advertises?

The real drop is measured from the peak price we've personally recorded for that exact listing down to its current price. The seller's advertised discount is measured against their own claimed original price. Neither is more official, but only one of them we can show happening, check by check.

Which item on this list has the longest tracking history?

The Lattafa Pride Shaheen perfume, 22 separate checks, sliding slowly from a $27.35 peak down to $23.42.

Does a small real drop, like the 3.6% on the CK One cologne, mean it's not a good deal?

Not necessarily. It means the tracked price movement was modest. The item can still clear this site's separate 50% off filter against the seller's own original price, that's a different measurement entirely.
