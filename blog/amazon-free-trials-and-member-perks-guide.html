Amazon Prime: The One Everything Else Builds On

Amazon Prime is Amazon's paid membership for free fast shipping, streaming and member-only deals. Most of the other perks on this list plug into it. Amazon's Prime sign-up page offers a free 30-day trial, and after that it's $14.99 a month or $139 a year. Paying yearly works out to about $11.58 a month.

What you get:

  • Fast, free delivery on millions of items, including Same-Day, One-Day and Two-Day options.
  • Prime Video, with new releases, Amazon Originals and live sports.
  • Prime Big Deal Days, member-only deals on October 6 and 7, 2026. Starting a trial now puts you inside the event.
  • Amazon Music with Prime, all the music and top podcasts ad-free.
  • Free same-day grocery delivery on $25+ orders in selected areas, plus member savings at Whole Foods Market.
  • Amazon Photos with unlimited full-resolution photo storage.
  • Fuel savings of 10 cents a gallon at more than 7,500 participating stations.
  • Grubhub+ at no extra cost, with $0 delivery fees on eligible orders.
  • Prescription savings at 60,000 pharmacies, including Walgreens, CVS and Amazon Pharmacy.

I use Prime myself. My own account's "Your Prime Journey" page says I saved $67 with Prime over the past 12 months, across 6 delivered orders. That's already more than four months of the monthly fee, and it doesn't count Prime Video or music.

The simple test: if free shipping alone saves you more than $14.99 in a month, Prime pays for itself before you touch Prime Video. And if you're about to order several items from our Amazon Picks, the trial covers the shipping on all of them.

Prime for Young Adults: 6 Months for $0

Prime for Young Adults is half-price Prime for people aged 18 to 24 and students. It's the best offer on this list, and a lot of people who qualify don't know it exists. Amazon's sign-up page gives you a 6-month trial for $0 with full Prime benefits. After that it's Prime at half the price: $7.49 a month or $69 a year.

It includes the same delivery, Prime Video, Prime Big Deal Days access and grocery benefits as regular Prime, plus 5% cash back on eligible items. Amazon notes the trial is courtesy of Grubhub.

If you have a younger sibling or a kid in college, send them this one.

Audible: Audiobooks for the Drive, the Gym and the Dishes

Audible is Amazon's audiobook service. Its standard membership gives you one audiobook of your choice every month, yours to keep, plus unlimited access to Audible's podcasts. Titles stay in your library even if you cancel.

Right now Audible is running a limited-time offer for new members: $0.99 a month for 4 months plus a $20 Audible credit. It auto-renews at $8.99 a month after the 4 months, and the offer ends October 7, 2026 at 11:59 PM PT. If you want more than one book a month, Audible Premium Plus is listed at $14.95 a month.

It fits anyone with dead time in their day. You can't turn pages while you drive, but you can press play.

Kindle Unlimited: A Library in Your Pocket

Kindle Unlimited is Amazon's ebook subscription. It gives you unlimited reading from more than 5 million digital titles, including best sellers, popular series and classics. It also includes thousands of audiobooks and a selection of magazines. You can read on a Kindle, your phone, a tablet or a computer, so you don't need a Kindle device.

It's $11.99 a month. Amazon's sign-up page is titled as a free trial offer, and the trial length varies, so check it there before you start.

Prime members also get Prime Reading, a smaller rotating selection of ebooks and magazines. If you run through that quickly, Kindle Unlimited is the step up.

Amazon Music Unlimited: Everything, Ad-Free

Amazon Music Unlimited is the full streaming catalog, a step above the music included with Prime. Amazon's page lists 100 million songs on demand, HD and Spatial Audio, a big library of Audible audiobooks and top podcasts ad-free.

The current offer for new subscribers is 4 months free with Prime, or 3 months free without it. After that it's $11.99 a month. Amazon marks it as a limited-time offer, so the length may change.

Subscribe & Save: Stop Paying Full Price for the Basics

Subscribe & Save is Amazon's automatic reorder program. It isn't a trial. It's free to use, and it quietly saves money on everything you rebuy. You pick an item, a quantity and a delivery schedule, and Amazon ships it automatically. The details below come from Amazon's Subscribe & Save page.

  • Save up to 15% when 5 or more subscribed products arrive in the same delivery to one address.
  • Shipping is always free.
  • Skip or cancel any time, and Amazon emails you before each delivery with the items and prices.

It works best for skincare, shampoo, vitamins, coffee, pet food and diapers. If you've read our simple dark spots routine, the sunscreen and serums in it are exactly the kind of thing to put on a schedule.

How to Try Everything Without Getting Charged

  • Set a calendar reminder 3 days before each trial ends. Every one of these renews automatically.
  • Cancel from your account. Every one of these can be canceled online from your Amazon account, and each sign-up page says "cancel anytime."
  • Check eligibility first. Most trials are for new members only. If you had Prime recently, you may not get another free trial right away.
  • Stack them. Prime, Audible and Kindle Unlimited are separate memberships, so you can try all three at once.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Amazon Prime free trial really free?

Yes. The 30-day trial costs nothing if you cancel before it ends. If you don't cancel, it renews at $14.99 a month or $139 a year, depending on the plan you picked.

Who qualifies for Prime for Young Adults?

People aged 18 to 24 and students. It starts with a 6-month trial for $0 and then costs $7.49 a month or $69 a year.

Do I keep my Audible books if I cancel?

Yes. Audiobooks you picked with your credits stay in your library after you cancel.

Is Kindle Unlimited included with Prime?

No. Prime includes Prime Reading, a smaller rotating selection. Kindle Unlimited is a separate $11.99 monthly membership with more than 5 million titles.

Can I use Subscribe & Save without Prime?

Yes. Subscribe & Save is open to everyone, and shipping on subscriptions is always free.

Do these deals change?

Often. Every price and trial length here was checked on Amazon's sign-up pages on September 24, 2026. The Audible and Amazon Business offers end in October, and Amazon can change any of them at any time.
