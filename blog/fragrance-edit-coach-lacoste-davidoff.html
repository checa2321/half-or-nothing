How to Read a Fragrance Pyramid

Every fragrance below comes with three layers of notes. Top notes are what you smell in the first few minutes, usually citrus, fruit or herbs. Heart notes arrive as the top fades and set the character, often florals, spices or woods. Base notes are what is left on your skin hours later, such as musk, amber, vetiver or sandalwood. When two sellers list slightly different notes for the same bottle, we point to the source so you can see where each list comes from.

Scent families help too. Aquatic and fresh scents smell like cool water, mint and clean air. Citrus and aromatic scents lean on lemon, grapefruit and herbs like lavender or rosemary. Floral scents center on jasmine, gardenia, rose or lily. Woody and musky scents finish warm, with cedar, vetiver, suede or sandalwood. Most perfumes mix two or three of these, which is why the guide above sorts by mood instead of by family.

When to Wear It

  • Office and everyday: Cool Water for him, Lacoste L.12.12 Blanc, Coach Signature and Lacoste Touch of Pink. They read clean and friendly, so they sit well in a shared space.
  • Warm weather: Cool Water for her, Coach Open Road and Lacoste Pour Femme. Light, watery and green notes feel natural when it is hot.
  • Dinner or a night out: Cool Water Intense and Coach Floral. Both have a bit more weight and warmth to them.
  • A gift: Cool Water is an easy, recognizable choice for him or for her. For someone who loves florals, Coach Signature or Lacoste Touch of Pink are safe, pretty options.

Before You Buy: A Few Things Worth Knowing

Eau de toilette and eau de parfum are different. An eau de parfum usually has a higher concentration of fragrance oils than an eau de toilette, which is why it often feels deeper. Both are real, full fragrances, so the right one is the one you like wearing.

Test it on your skin. The same perfume can smell a little different from one person to the next. If you can, try a sample or a counter tester of the same scent first.

Store it well. Keep bottles away from heat and direct sunlight, in a cool, dry place.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which fragrance is best for everyday wear?

It depends on the feel you want. For clean and easy, Davidoff Cool Water and Lacoste L.12.12 Blanc are the lighter picks. For something a little warmer, Coach Signature and Coach Floral lean elegant.

What is the difference between EDT and EDP?

Eau de toilette (EDT) and eau de parfum (EDP) are both finished fragrances. An EDP typically contains more fragrance oil than an EDT, so it often smells richer. The labels are a guide to concentration, not to quality.

How can I make my perfume last longer?

Spray it on moisturized skin, at pulse points such as the wrists and the sides of the neck, and avoid rubbing your wrists together afterward. How long a scent lasts still depends on the formula and on your skin.

Which of these would make a good gift?

Cool Water is the easiest, because the scent is so widely recognized. For a floral lover, Coach Signature, Coach Floral and Lacoste Touch of Pink are all approachable. If you are unsure, pick the fresher scent over the warmer one.

Should I choose EDT or EDP if I am buying online?

If you already know you like a scent, either works. If you are trying something new, an eau de toilette is usually lighter and more forgiving, while an eau de parfum is usually richer. Check the size too, since the same fragrance can come in several.

Are any of these listed as testers?
