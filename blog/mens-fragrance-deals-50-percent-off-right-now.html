Why the names on this list might surprise you

If you're expecting a page of Dior and Tom Ford at half price, that's not what 50%+ off men's fragrance actually looks like on eBay right now. The deepest, most reliable discounts come from smaller houses (Lomani, Risala, Maison Alhambra, Armaf, Glenn Perri) alongside a handful of licensed novelty scents. That's not a lesser list, it's just a different one. These brands don't carry the markup that lets a luxury name “discount” 50% and still turn a profit at retail. When a smaller house clears inventory, the cut is real and it goes deep, several of the deals below are 84% off or steeper. If you want the same story for Middle Eastern houses specifically, see who Lattafa, Rasasi and Al Haramain actually are.

Most of what's below is EDT or EDP, the two concentrations that make up nearly every men's fragrance listing: EDT (eau de toilette) is the lighter, more diluted pour, EDP (eau de parfum) carries a higher concentration of fragrance oil and lasts longer on skin.
