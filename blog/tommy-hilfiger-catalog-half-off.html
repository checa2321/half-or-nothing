Tommy Hilfiger has more real inventory on Half or Nothing right now than almost any other brand we track, and it's concentrated in one place: tailoring. Sport coats, suit separates, and dress pants make up most of what's live, all from the same seller, all new with tags, all 50% off or more against the real list price on the tag.

If tailoring specifically is what brought you here, our Fall 2026 Blazer Edit covers five more designer jackets at similar discounts.

Tommy Hilfiger launched in New York in 1985 with a preppy, Ivy-inspired menswear line. The brand's own label for its look, "classic American cool," still describes what these pieces are: East Coast prep tailoring with a more relaxed, casual edge than a European suit house would put out. The flag on the tag traces back to the International Code of Signals flag for the letter H, a nod to the brand's nautical roots rather than a random graphic (source: Tommy Hilfiger company history). PVH Corp. (then Phillips-Van Heusen) bought the company in 2010 and still owns it today. PVH also owns Calvin Klein, another brand with real inventory on Half or Nothing, covered in our Calvin Klein deal guide.

What to Check Before You Buy

A few things worth confirming before checkout on any of these:

  • Size availability. Most of these listings are single sizes or a small run of sizes, not open stock. Check the listing directly for the size you need before assuming it's still there.
  • New with tags versus pre-owned. Every item in this catalog is listed new with tags, not a pre-owned or floor-sample piece, but always confirm the listing condition yourself before buying.
  • The real list price. Every discount above is calculated against the price actually printed on the tag or shown as the retailer's list price on the listing, not an inflated "compare at" number.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who owns Tommy Hilfiger now?

PVH Corp. (formerly Phillips-Van Heusen) has owned Tommy Hilfiger since acquiring the company in March 2010. PVH also owns Calvin Klein, another brand with a real presence on Half or Nothing.

What does the flag on the logo mean?

It traces back to the International Code of Signals flag for the letter H, a maritime signal flag rather than an invented graphic, which fits the brand's long-running nautical, East Coast prep styling.

Is this the real Tommy Hilfiger brand or a diffusion line?

Almost everything in this catalog is mainline Tommy Hilfiger. Two pieces are noted separately: the Floral Chiffon Mini Dress is from the Tommy Hilfiger Jeans line, a real Tommy Hilfiger sub-label rather than a different company.

Why are the sport coats discounted so much more than the dress pants?

Sport coats and suit jackets typically carry higher list prices to begin with, $295 to $450 in this catalog versus $95 to $190 for dress pants, so the same dollar markdown reads as a much larger percentage off.

Are these new or pre-owned?

Every item listed here is new with tags as of the date we checked. Condition can change listing to listing over time, so always confirm on the actual eBay page before buying.

Can I return any of these if the size doesn't work?

Return policy is set by the individual eBay seller, not by Half or Nothing. Check the specific listing's return policy before buying, especially on single-size pieces.

How often does this catalog get updated?

Prices and stock on eBay listings change without notice. We verify prices at the time we publish, but always double-check the current price on the actual listing before buying.
