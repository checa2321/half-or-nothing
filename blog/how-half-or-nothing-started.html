How Half or Nothing Started

2026-08-26 · Written by Dann Nazario · 5 min read

There's one thing we can all agree on when it comes to shopping: a discount is always welcome. The problem is that the discounts that are actually worth it are surprisingly hard to find.

For a long time, I searched for them myself. I would walk into stores, browse websites, compare prices, and spend way too much time trying to find something that actually felt like a great buy. And most of the time, what I found was… pretty disappointing.

Three dollars off. Six dollars. Maybe nine.

And yes, technically, that's a discount. But it was never the kind of discount that made me stop and think: wow, now that's a great buy.

I wanted the kind of deal that makes you look at the original price, look at the current price, and think: how is this actually selling for this price?

Because we also know that we often end up paying much more for things than they're actually worth. There are a thousand reasons for it: the brand, the season, the name, the packaging, the marketing, the fact that something is new, or simply because we want it badly enough.

But that's another conversation.

What I wanted was much simpler: I wanted to find things that were actually worth buying, at prices that were actually worth paying.

So I started searching. And searching. And searching.

I spent hours on the same websites we all use, going through page after page of products and offers, hoping to find something that wasn't simply marked down a few dollars. Most of the time, it felt like looking for a needle in a haystack.

And eventually I thought:

Why am I searching through all of this when I already know exactly what I want to see?

I don't want to see a $3, $6, or $9 discount.

I don't want to spend hours scrolling through hundreds of products just to find, if I'm lucky, one that's actually worth it.

I want at least 50% off. I want 70%. I want 80%. I want the kind of discount that makes you say, “Okay… now that's a deal.”

And that's when I realized something important: not everything that's on sale is actually a deal.

Because we've all been there: you see an incredible discount, get excited, and then when you go to check out, you discover that the price only applies if you buy something else, become a member, use a specific payment method, meet some condition buried somewhere in the fine print, or that there's simply a completely different story behind the price that looked so good. That's exactly the kind of gap how we verify every deal is built to catch.

And in the end, it wasn't what it looked like.

That's why, to me, finding a real deal also means looking beyond the percentage displayed on the screen. Just because something has been marked down doesn't automatically mean it's worth buying. For me, several things have to line up: the product has to be good, the price has to make sense, and the discount has to be significant enough to actually change the buying decision.

It's not simply about finding things at a price that looks unbelievable.

It's about finding genuinely good things that are actually worth having, at prices you wouldn't normally expect to see.

I kept telling Che this over and over, until eventually the idea came up.

Why don't we find these deals ourselves?

Not just for me, but for anyone else who doesn't want to spend hours looking for them either.

And that's how Half or Nothing was born.

The idea was clear: do the searching ourselves, filter through all the noise, and bring the offers that are actually worth it together in one place.

And we don't search just one store. We search across a variety of stores and platforms, because the best opportunities can show up anywhere. That selection will continue to grow over time as we discover new places, new brands, and new opportunities that truly deserve to be here.

Sometimes it will be a piece from a famous brand selling for a fraction of its original price. Other times, it might be a brand you've never heard of, but the product is genuinely good and the discount is almost ridiculous.

The brand doesn't have to be famous, although it probably will be.

What matters is that the product and the opportunity are genuinely worth it.

Because Half or Nothing isn't about buying something simply because it's on sale.

It's about finding that moment when the price finally makes sense.

That moment when you don't feel like you found something cheap, but rather something good at an extraordinary price.

We do the searching. We do the filtering. You find the deal.

You save time. You spend less. You find better things.

And now that we're finding the opportunities for you, you save time and money.

That's Half or Nothing.

Frequently asked questions

Why does Half or Nothing require at least 50% off instead of any discount?

Because a $3 or $6 discount, while technically real, was never the kind of deal worth stopping for. The site was built around looking for the discount depth that actually changes a buying decision, not just any markdown.

Does Half or Nothing only feature famous brands?

No. The brand doesn't have to be famous. What matters is that the product and the discount are both genuinely worth it, whether that's a well-known name at a fraction of its price or a brand you've never heard of with a real discount behind it.

Which stores does Half or Nothing search?

More than one. The site searches across a variety of stores and platforms rather than a single source, since the best opportunities can show up anywhere, and that selection keeps growing.

What kind of "deals" does Half or Nothing try to avoid?

The kind where the advertised price only applies if you buy something else, join a membership, use a specific payment method, or meet a condition buried in the fine print. That gap between the advertised price and the real one is exactly what the site's verification process is built to catch.
