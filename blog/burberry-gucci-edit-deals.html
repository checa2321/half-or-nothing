How to build the look

Pick one bag, not both, the check for daytime, the black for everything that needs to look serious. Carry the wallet inside either one, it is doing more work than its size suggests. Let the sunglasses and the pouch travel together, small, quiet, replaceable pieces that still read as considered. Wear the scent daily, it costs the least and shows up the most. That restraint, more than any single price tag, is what makes an edit look expensive instead of accumulated.

Before you buy: a designer resale checklist

Leather goods and eyewear from major houses are among the most counterfeited categories anywhere, so this part deserves real attention regardless of which piece you're after.

  • For bags and wallets, look for a serial number, date code, or authenticity card referenced directly in the listing.
  • For sunglasses, genuine pairs stamp the model and serial number on the inside of the arm, check that the listing photos show it.
  • Check that hardware stamping, the brand name engraved into zippers, clasps, or buttons, is visible in the listing photos.
  • Ask about the dust bag or box. Its presence isn't proof on its own, but its total absence on a piece at this price is worth a direct question before you buy.
  • Same rule as always: check seller feedback and return policy before checkout.
