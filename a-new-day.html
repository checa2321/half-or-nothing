How We Verify These Deals

Every A New Day listing is checked against the retailer's own stated original price before it counts as a real markdown. Read the full verification methodology for exactly what that check does and doesn't confirm.

Why 50% and Not Less

Plenty of A New Day markdowns clear 10% or 20% off. Half or Nothing only lists the ones at 50% off or more, because that is the threshold where a discount stops being routine pricing noise and starts being worth a second look. A listing under that line never makes the page, no matter how small the seller or how large the catalogue.

A New Day FAQ

Is the A New Day discount on Half or Nothing real?

Yes. Every discount is checked against the retailer's own stated original price before a deal clears the 50% off threshold. Deals that fail this check never make the page. See our full verification methodology.

How often are A New Day deals updated?

The A New Day list is refreshed hourly from eBay. Deals that no longer clear 50% off are removed within the hour.
