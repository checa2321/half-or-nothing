What to Check Before You Buy

Makeup is where a low price is easiest to misread, so check three things before you buy.

First, condition. Listings say new, sealed, tester or open box, and they are not the same. A tester is a genuine product without retail packaging, which is fine for many buyers but is not a sealed box. Read the condition line and look at the photos for a safety seal.

Second, freshness. Products carry a period-after-opening symbol, the small open jar with a number of months, and many carry a batch code that lookup sites can turn into a manufacture date. Mascara and liquid products age fastest. Powders last longest.

Third, shade. Foundation and concealer shade names are not consistent across brands, and screens change colors. If the shade is a guess, look for a seller with easy returns, keeping in mind that many sellers do not accept returns on opened cosmetics.

Because these products touch your skin, buy from sellers who show the actual item. We compare each price to the seller's own stated original price. See how we verify every deal.
