What to Check Before You Buy

Sunglasses are easy to get wrong when you shop by price alone, so here is what matters before you buy.

UV protection is the part that protects your eyes. Look for a listing that says UV400 or 100% UVA/UVB protection. Polarized lenses are a different feature: they cut glare from water and roads, but polarization on its own says nothing about UV blocking, and a dark tint does not mean UV protection either.

Fit matters as much as the brand. Frames list three numbers in millimeters, usually lens width, bridge and temple length, for example 55-18-140. If you own a pair that fits, match those numbers.

Designer sunglasses attract counterfeits on every marketplace. Check that the listing shows the model number printed inside the temple, includes the original case, and comes from a seller with a return policy you can use. We compare each price to the seller's own stated original price and we do not verify authenticity, so treat the seller's history and photos as part of the decision.
