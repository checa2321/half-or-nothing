How We Verify These Deals

Every Retrofête listing is checked against the retailer's own stated original price before it counts as a real markdown. Read the full verification methodology for exactly what that check does and doesn't confirm.

Why 50% and Not Less

Plenty of Retrofête markdowns clear 10% or 20% off. Half or Nothing only lists the ones at 50% off or more, because that is the threshold where a discount stops being routine pricing noise and starts being worth a second look. A listing under that line never makes the page, no matter how small the seller or how large the catalogue.

Retrofête FAQ

Is the Retrofête discount on Half or Nothing real?

Yes. Every discount is checked against the retailer's own stated original price before a deal clears the 50% off threshold. Deals that fail this check never make the page. See our full verification methodology.

How often are Retrofête deals updated?

The Retrofête list is refreshed hourly from eBay. Deals that no longer clear 50% off are removed within the hour.
