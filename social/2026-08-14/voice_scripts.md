# Voice scripts — what a human says over the carousel/video

Timed to the slides: COVER → ITEM 1-6 → CTA. Read at a normal fast pace, total lands around 25-30s.

---

# POST A — "We almost didn't post this" (fragancias)

## ENGLISH

HOOK — (0-3s, over COVER slide)
"Okay, we almost didn't post this one."

LOOP_POINT — (~3s)
Trail off right there, don't explain yet. Cut straight into the next line so it feels like you caught yourself mid-thought.

REHOOK — (before 10s, over ITEM 1-2)
"Because three of these are actual elixir concentrates and nobody's fixed the price yet. Tous, Carolina Herrera, Mont Blanc, all sitting past sixty-five percent off."

BODY — (over ITEM 3-6)
"And the same sellers are quietly clearing three more fragrances right now. Ajmal's Aurum for thirty-one bucks instead of three-fifty. The Thierry Mugler Angel Elixir, the star-shaped bottle, at sixty-five percent off. Guess Seductive Red for under fifteen dollars.
Oh, and small thing, we started picking Amazon finds too. Personal ones. Not saying more yet."

SAVE_TRIGGER — (over CTA)
"Save this one right now, not later. These sellers restock fast and the second this gets seen, the price moves. Full list's at bio.link/halfornothing."

# POST B — "Swipe before they fix the price" (mixto)

"They have not fixed the price on these yet."

LOOP_POINT — (~3s)
Hold the phone up like you're showing proof, don't say what "these" are yet.

REHOOK — (before 10s, over ITEM 1-2)
"Designer sunglasses for under sixty-five bucks, twice. Ferragamo, both of them, eighty-six percent off."

"Calvin Klein sunglasses at twenty-six dollars. Real Egyptian cotton sheets, one thousand thread count, for thirty-nine. Kenneth Cole dress pants at eleven forty-seven. A pair of Memphis Audio speakers at ninety percent off.
And every single price here is checked against our own history, not whatever the seller says the 'original' price was.
Also, quietly, we started picking Amazon finds too. First real drop's coming soon."

"Save this before it's gone, most of these sellers never restock the same item twice. Everything's at bio.link/halfornothing."
