How We Verify These Deals

Why 50% and Not Less

Plenty of Citizen markdowns clear 10% or 20% off. Half or Nothing only lists the ones at 50% off or more, because that is the threshold where a discount stops being routine pricing noise and starts being worth a second look. A listing under that line never makes the page, no matter how small the seller or how large the catalogue.
