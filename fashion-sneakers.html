What to Check Before You Buy

A sneaker deal only counts if the pair fits and is real.

Sizing first: the same number means different things across brands, and men's, women's and youth sizes are not interchangeable. Compare the listed size against the brand's own chart, and when the listing gives a length in centimeters, trust that over the label.

Authenticity next. Genuine pairs carry a style code on the tag inside the shoe, usually a mix of letters and numbers, that you can search to confirm the model and colorway. Mismatched codes, misspelled labels, or a listing that only shows a stock photo and no photos of the real pair are reasons to walk away.

Then condition. New with box, new without box and pre-owned all appear here, and the gap between them is often most of the price difference. Prefer listings that show the actual shoes from several angles and a return policy you can use.

The discount shown is measured against the seller's own stated original price, not a market average, and we do not verify authenticity. Read how we verify each deal.
