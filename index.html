Trust & How It Works

What Our Price Tracking Actually Proves (And What It Doesn't)

Clear answers

Good finds deserve a clear standard.

Half or Nothing finds online deals that are at least 50% off the retailer's own stated price. Browse live discounts across fashion, electronics, home and kitchen, beauty, fragrances, jewelry, watches, outdoor gear and more. eBay listings are rescanned hourly and removed the moment they no longer clear the 50% bar. Amazon Picks are a separate, hand-checked section, not pulled automatically. Every deal links straight to the retailer so you can confirm the current price before buying. See how we verify every deal.

How does Half or Nothing choose which deals to list?

Every eBay listing on the site is pulled hourly through eBay's official Browse API and checked against the retailer's own stated original price. Only listings at 50% off or more clear the bar; anything under that never makes the page, no matter how small the seller. Amazon Picks are a separate, hand-curated section: no live Amazon API exists for this, so each pick is chosen and price-checked individually rather than pulled automatically. See the full verification methodology.

How often are prices verified?

The eBay listings rescan automatically every hour, and anything that no longer clears 50% off is removed within the hour. Amazon Picks don't have an automated feed to rescan, so those prices and coupons are re-checked by hand rather than on a fixed schedule.

What qualifies a deal for the page?

A real 50% off or more against the seller's own original listing, confirmed at verification time, no matter how few units are in stock or how small the seller. Fake countdown timers, coupon-only "discounts" presented as the listed price, and inflated reference prices are the kinds of things that disqualify a listing, not seller size.

Are the links on this site affiliate links?

Yes. Half or Nothing earns a commission through the eBay Partner Network and Amazon Associates when someone buys through a link here, at no extra cost to the buyer. That commission never decides which deals get listed or how they're ranked; a listing has to clear the discount bar first, on its own. See how the site is paid for.

Is the discount on Half or Nothing real?

Yes. Every discount is checked against the retailer's own stated original price before a deal clears the 50% off threshold. Deals that fail this check never make the page. See our full verification methodology.

How often are deals updated?

The list is refreshed hourly from eBay. Deals that no longer clear 50% off are removed within the hour.

eBay & Amazon

eBay. Rescanned hourly. Sorted purely by discount, and nothing under 50% is listed. No countdown timers, no invented scarcity: every number on this page is measured, not staged. See How We Verify Deals for the full methodology.

Amazon works differently. The Amazon Picks section is hand-picked by Che, one at a time. When a pick shows a price, list price, or coupon, it was verified by hand directly on Amazon -- not pulled from an automated scan -- see About for why.

Affiliate disclosure & how this page is built

Some links on this site are affiliate links. This site is a member of the eBay Partner Network. If you buy through one, I may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. Commission never affects which deals are listed or the order they appear in. Listings default to newest first; sort by discount, price, or name any time. Original prices and discount percentages are set by the seller and shown as reported by the retailer's API; Half or Nothing does not independently verify them. As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases.

Pricing & how often this updates

Prices and availability change at the retailer. Whatever the retailer shows at the time of purchase is what applies.

The "% off" shown on each card is the discount the seller lists against their own stated original price. It comes straight from the retailer's data, not from our own price history, and we don't independently verify it.

This run fetched: 6466 ebay. Green marks listings new since the previous run. Most listings come off the page unless they have been re-confirmed at their retailer within the last 6 hours. Three kinds are deliberately held longer: our priority sellers, our Beauty and Fashion listings (both rotate through the scan less often than the window allows), and any deal we have linked to from a social post, which is checked against the retailer one by one before each update instead. Every card shows when it was last seen, so the real age is always on the page.
